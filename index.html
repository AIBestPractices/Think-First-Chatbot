Why this is a good way to use AI

AI can help people learn, as long as it supports their thinking instead of replacing it.

A tutor, not an answer machine

The robot only responds once you've shared your reasoning, so the work of thinking stays with you.

Hints before answers

Guiding prompts help you find the next step yourself, so a mistake turns into learning instead of a dead end.

Understanding you keep

Explaining your approach shows what you know and what's missing, and it makes the idea easier to remember.

Check it, don't just trust it

The robot gives a small example you can verify yourself. Good AI use means checking answers instead of accepting them blindly.
